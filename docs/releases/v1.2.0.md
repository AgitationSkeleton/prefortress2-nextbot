### Nav repairs at map load
Found by looking for spots where bots spend a large share of their time without moving (server heatmaps).

- **Gap stitching** (`pf2nb_stitch_gaps`, on by default; `pf2nb_stitches` lists them): the nav generator drops samples that land on stair risers, which can leave a one-cell hole that cuts a spawn off. At map load, facing areas up to about two cells apart are linked when the floor across is continuous (steps, stairs and ramps, never a ledge) and a player fits. bam4's spiral stairs had cut off both spawns (bots could reach 13% of the mesh and every flag fetcher stood in spawn; now 98%), and pl_jungle_a1c's ship-hold stairs had trapped BLU's hold spawns.
- **Teleporters** (`tf_bot_pf2_teleports`, on by default; `pf2nb_teleports` lists them): each client `trigger_teleport` becomes a one-way nav link to its destination. The path cost honours the trigger's team filter and enabled state, and bots walk into the trigger. plr_highertower's Civilian spawn room, whose only exit is a teleporter, no longer keeps Civilians in it; cp_chaoscanyon_a7 went from 0% to 97% reachable and cp_labor_pf2 from 26% to 99.8%.
- **`pf2nb_reach`**: from each team's spawns, which flags and control points the mesh reaches.
- `pf2nb_nav_generate` also seeds from flags, capture zones and pickups.

### Known gaps
- Lifts (`func_door` / `func_movelinear`) are not nav links: on ctf_well_classic_b2 the flag rooms are reachable only by lifts.
- Do not hot-reload the plugin (`meta unload`/`load` followed by a level change crashes in the engine); restart the server instead.
