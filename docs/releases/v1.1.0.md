### Fixes
- **Bots hopping in a spawn corner:** door triggers that stop short of their door (ctf_2fort_classic_pf2) made the owning team's spawn exit count as blocked for everyone, and the bots then ran a straight line at the goal into the spawn corner. Fixed with a trigger margin; a failed path also no longer falls back to a straight line at a far goal. Stuck events on that map went from about 1000 to single digits per 150 s.
- **Impossible mesh links:** at map load every connection is walked with a player hull, and links no player can cross (ledges too high, railings, thin walls) are skipped (`pf2nb_prune_walls`).
- Map-wide round-end `trigger_hurt` volumes (Stonehenge) are no longer treated as hazards (the VIP stood still for minutes).

### Hunted
- The VIP moves with its team instead of waiting at the spawn for the gate, takes a route that varies per life, and waits briefly when far ahead.
- RED snipers roam vantage points near the VIP instead of lurking at their spawn.

### New
- Optional `pf2_hunted_models` SourceMod plugin: on `hunted_actually_accurate*`, the recoloured sniper model (`models/player/sniper_ylw.mdl`) stays on RED snipers only.
