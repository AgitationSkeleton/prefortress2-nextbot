### Fixes
- Stonehenge (`hunted_stonehenge_test`): bots no longer stay in BLU's spawn. At map load, long drops that can't actually land on the area below are skipped (`pf2nb_prune_drops`; list them with `pf2nb_drops`).

### Performance
- Much smoother servers with many bots: cached entity field offsets, team and game-rules lookups. With 22 bots, average path search time went from 2.0 to 0.18 ms, and frames with over 20 ms of bot thinking from 550 to 7 in a test run.
- `pf2nb_perf [reset]` profiles server frames, to tell bot load apart from other stutter.

### Debug
- `pf2nb_path <bot>`: a bot's current path.
